Haven

The challenge

Most real estate sites feel like spreadsheets. Haven needed to feel aspirational and still be easy to use.

Making real estate feel premium.

Real estate sites often cram in listings, filters and prices with no sense of brand. I wanted each listing to lead with its photography and still surface the right homes fast.

Each visual choice had to answer one question: does this make the property more desirable, or does it get in the way?

Process

  1. Research and direction

    I studied premium real estate and hospitality sites and noted what makes them feel high-end: whitespace, large photos, restrained type, warm palettes.

  2. Search and filter UX

    I mapped how a buyer searches (location, price, type, features) and designed filters that feel natural instead of clinical.

  3. Listing cards

    Each card leads with the image. Clear type hierarchy and consistent spacing keep browsing smooth. Built with Next.js and TypeScript.

  4. Responsive and deployment

    Mobile-first, since many property searches happen on phones. I tuned image sizes and card layouts per breakpoint and deployed to Vercel.
